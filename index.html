"The menu is not a weight loss menu — but it can work for weight loss if you choose the right dishes and control portions. Most of your high-cal items are heavy in oil, fried elements, or refined carbs."

About 8 out of 26 dishes are genuinely weight-loss friendly as-is. The rest need portion adjustments or swaps.
01
Replace fried plantain with grilled plantain wherever possible — saves 80–120 kcal per serving.
02
For pasta dishes, reduce portion size to 1 cup cooked and add a side salad or cucumber to bulk it out.
03
Custard has hidden sugar. Ask chef to reduce sugar or switch to plain yoghurt as a base.
04
Jollof and fried rice: half portion with extra protein (grilled chicken) keeps you full without the calorie load.
05
Bole & fish and beans & plantain are your best filling low-calorie options. Rotate them frequently.
06
Breakfast matters most. Grilled chicken + baked potatoes is the strongest breakfast on this menu for a cut.
